A SCHOOL PROJECT BUILT WITH CURIOSITY

About <shakcode>

We started with no app-making experience. With planning, teamwork, and help from AI, we learned as we built.

YOUR STORY

About us

<shakcode> began as our school computer science project. At first, we had no idea how to start or how to make an app. Akilesh volunteered the project to our teacher, and Shravan, Nithesh, Sharvesh, and Vijayaragavan joined him to form a team of five.

As the project went on, circumstances meant some teammates had to step away. Shravan and Akilesh planned the work and completed the app together.

We made <shakcode> as an experiment in what students can create through computer science. We began without knowing how to build an app, then learned, tried ideas, and made one ourselves. An honourable mention goes to ChatGPT for helping us with ideas, planning, and building the app.

THE IDEA THAT INSPIRED US

“In the modern world, AI won’t take your job; the person who knows how to use AI better than you might.”
